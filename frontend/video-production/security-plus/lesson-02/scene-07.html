Identity Management & Account Security

Secure identity management controls the entire lifecycle of an account—from creation and authentication to access changes and eventual removal.

Identity Lifecycle Management
IDENTITY MANAGEMENT

Manage the Entire Lifecycle

An identity is more than a username. Organizations must manage accounts throughout their lifecycle and ensure that access remains appropriate as a user's responsibilities change.

Lifecycle Management Create, modify, review, disable, and remove accounts when appropriate.
Account Security Protect identities from misuse, compromise, and unnecessary privileges.
ACCOUNT LIFECYCLE

From Creation to Removal

Create

Establish an identity and assign only the access required for the user's initial responsibilities.

START Provision securely
Modify

Update permissions when roles, responsibilities, or business requirements change.

CHANGE Adjust access
Remove

Disable or remove accounts when access is no longer required.

END Deprovision promptly
IDENTITY LIFECYCLE MODEL

Joiner, Mover & Leaver

Identity management should follow people throughout their relationship with an organization. A new employee, a user changing roles, and a departing employee each require different access management actions.

Joiners receive appropriate access. Movers have access reviewed and adjusted. Leavers should have unnecessary access removed promptly.
ACCOUNT SECURITY

Different Accounts, Different Risk

Standard Account

Used for normal day-to-day work with permissions appropriate to regular responsibilities.

Privileged Account

Provides elevated capabilities and must be carefully controlled and monitored.

Service Account

Used by applications or services and should receive only the permissions required for their function.

ACCOUNT PROTECTION

Protect Valuable Identities

Account security reduces the chance that attackers can take control of an identity and use its permissions. Strong authentication and careful privilege management work together to reduce identity-related risk.

Use strong authentication, protect privileged accounts, monitor suspicious activity, and remove unnecessary access.
ACCESS GOVERNANCE

Review Access Regularly

Verify

Confirm that the identity belongs to an active and legitimate user, service, or process.

Evaluate

Determine whether assigned permissions remain appropriate for current responsibilities.

Correct

Remove unnecessary permissions and correct access that exceeds legitimate business needs.

MEMORY CHECK

Secure Identity Lifecycle

CREATE Establish identity
USE Authenticate & authorize
REVIEW Validate access
REMOVE End unnecessary access
PROVISION Create appropriate access
REVIEW Keep access appropriate
DEPROVISION Remove unnecessary access
KEY TAKEAWAY Secure identity management follows an account throughout its entire lifecycle. Access should be provisioned carefully, adjusted when responsibilities change, reviewed regularly, and removed when no longer needed. The goal is simple: every identity should have the right access—no more and no less.
