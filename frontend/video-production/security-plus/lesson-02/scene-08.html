One Security Question, Four Connected Concepts

Secure systems need to know who is requesting access, verify that identity, determine what the identity is allowed to do, and manage that access throughout the account lifecycle.

SECURITY PRINCIPLES

The Rules Behind Secure Access

Least Privilege

Give identities only the access required to perform legitimate tasks.

Need to Know

Access to sensitive information should be limited to legitimate business requirements.

Accountability

Actions should be associated with identities so activity can be monitored and investigated.
