Access Control & Authorization

Learn how authorization determines what an authenticated subject can access, modify, or perform.

Identity & Access Management
AUTHORIZATION

What Can You Do?

Authorization determines what an authenticated user, device, process, or service is permitted to access or perform. Authentication establishes identity. Authorization applies permissions to that identity.

Authenticated Identity First establish who or what is requesting access.
Permissions Then determine which actions are allowed.
ACCESS CONTROL MODELS

Common Authorization Models

DAC

Discretionary Access Control allows the owner of a resource to control access to it.

KEY IDEA Owner decides
MAC

Mandatory Access Control uses centrally defined security labels and rules.

KEY IDEA Labels + policy
RBAC

Role-Based Access Control assigns permissions according to a user's organizational role.

KEY IDEA Role → permissions
ACCESS CONTROL MODEL

Attribute-Based Access Control

ABAC evaluates attributes associated with the subject, resource, action, and environment to determine whether access should be permitted.

Example: allow access when the user, device, location, resource, and requested action satisfy policy.
AUTHORIZATION

Permissions & Privileges

Read

View or retrieve information without necessarily changing the resource.

Write

Create, modify, or update information.

Execute

Run a program, command, process, or permitted action.

PRIVILEGE MANAGEMENT

Control Elevated Access

Privileged accounts can perform sensitive administrative actions. These accounts should be carefully controlled, monitored, and limited to legitimate business needs.

Apply least privilege, separate administrative accounts when appropriate, and review elevated access regularly.
ACCOUNT MANAGEMENT

Access Lifecycle

Provision

Create an account and assign the access required for the user's responsibilities.

Review

Verify that assigned access remains appropriate as responsibilities change.

Deprovision

Remove access when it is no longer required, such as after termination or role changes.

MEMORY CHECK

Authorization Flow

IDENTITY Who are you?
POLICY What rule applies?
PERMISSION What can you do?
DECISION Allow or deny
AUTHENTICATE Establish identity
AUTHORIZE Apply permissions
CONTROL Allow or deny access
KEY TAKEAWAY Authentication answers “Who are you?” Authorization answers “What are you allowed to do?” Access control models such as DAC, MAC, RBAC, and ABAC apply different rules for making authorization decisions. Effective access management also includes controlling privileged access and removing permissions when they are no longer needed.
