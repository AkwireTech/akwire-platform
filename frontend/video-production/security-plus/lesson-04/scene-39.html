Backup Strategies & Recovery Planning

Effective recovery depends on reliable backups, appropriate backup methods, protected recovery copies, and regular testing to ensure critical data can actually be restored.

THE RECOVERY FOUNDATION

Recovery begins with usable backups

A recovery plan is only effective when the organization has reliable copies of important data and can restore those copies within the required recovery objectives.

BACKUP METHODS Understand what each backup captures
Full Copies all selected data during the backup operation.
Incremental Copies data changed since the previous backup.
Differential Copies data changed since the last full backup.
Snapshot Captures a point-in-time representation of a system or dataset.
RECOVERY WORKFLOW A backup is useful only when it can be restored
01 Identify Determine critical systems and data.
02 Back Up Create protected recovery copies.
03 Test Verify that data can be restored.
04 Restore Recover systems when needed.
BACKUP SECURITY Protect recovery copies from the same threat
OFFLINE Isolated Copies
IMMUTABLE Protected Copies
OFFSITE Separate Location
If attackers can reach the backup system, they may be able to encrypt, delete, or corrupt the recovery copies as well.
BACKUP STRATEGY Build multiple layers of recoverability
3 COPIES Maintain multiple copies of important data.
2 MEDIA TYPES Use different storage media or systems.
1 OFFSITE Keep at least one copy separated from the primary location.
RECOVERY PRACTICE Validate the recovery process before an incident
Schedule Define backup frequency and retention.
Test Perform restoration tests regularly.
Measure Confirm recovery meets the required objectives.
Document Record procedures and recovery results.
EXAM MEMORY

Backups are not recovery until restoration is proven.

Remember the difference between creating a backup and demonstrating that the organization can successfully restore the required data within its recovery objectives.

SCENE SUMMARY Four ideas to remember
Backup Types Full, incremental, differential, and snapshots serve different purposes.
Restoration Backups must be tested to prove recovery works.
Protection Isolate and protect backups from the threats affecting production systems.
Strategy Multiple copies and locations improve recoverability.
AKWIRE SECURITY+ CORE CONCEPT

A backup is valuable only when it can be trusted and restored.

Build layered backups, protect recovery copies, test restoration, and measure recovery against the organization's objectives.
