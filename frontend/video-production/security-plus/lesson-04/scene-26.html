Data Protection Fundamentals

Protecting data requires understanding where information exists, how sensitive it is, how it moves, and which security controls should be applied throughout its lifecycle.

LESSON 04 • DATA PROTECTION

Data must be protected throughout its lifecycle

Effective data protection begins with identifying the information that needs protection and applying controls from creation through storage, transmission, use, and disposal.

DATA STATES Understand where data exists
Data at Rest Information stored on devices, databases, servers, or other storage systems.
Data in Transit Information moving between systems, networks, applications, or users.
Data in Use Information actively being processed by applications, systems, or users.
Archived Data Information retained for operational, legal, regulatory, or business requirements.
DATA LIFECYCLE Protect information from creation to disposal
Create Information enters the organization.
Store Protect information in storage.
Use Control access during processing.
Dispose Remove information securely when no longer needed.
DATA CLASSIFICATION Classification helps determine the required protection
LOW SENSITIVITY Public
ORGANIZATIONAL Internal
HIGH SENSITIVITY Sensitive
The more sensitive the information, the stronger and more carefully controlled the protection should be.
PROTECTION CONTROLS Use multiple controls to protect sensitive information
RISK Unprotected Data Excessive access, weak storage protection, insecure transmission, and improper disposal can expose information.
VS
PROTECTION Protected Data Classification, access controls, encryption, monitoring, retention, and secure disposal reduce exposure.
DATA PROTECTION PRACTICE Four questions to ask when protecting information
What is it? Identify the type and sensitivity of the data.
Where is it? Determine whether data is stored, moving, or in use.
Who needs it? Apply appropriate access and authorization controls.
How is it removed? Follow retention and secure disposal requirements.
EXAM MEMORY

Classify → Locate → Control → Retain → Dispose

When a Security+ scenario involves data protection, determine what the information is, where it exists, who should access it, how long it should be retained, and how it should eventually be disposed of.

LESSON 04 • SCENE 26 SUMMARY Data protection starts with understanding the data
Identify and classify information according to sensitivity.
Protect data at rest, in transit, and in use.
Apply appropriate access and protection controls.
Retain information appropriately and dispose of it securely.
