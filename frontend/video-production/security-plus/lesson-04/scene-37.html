Recovery Testing & Validation

ACT 01 • RECOVERY TESTING

Recovery plans must be tested before a real disaster

A recovery plan may look effective on paper but still fail during an actual disruption. Regular testing validates procedures, identifies weaknesses, and helps ensure systems and data can be restored.

ACT 02 • TEST TYPES

Recovery exercises can vary in depth

Tabletop Exercise Teams discuss how they would respond to a scenario
Walkthrough Participants review recovery procedures step by step
Simulation A realistic scenario is used to evaluate response
Full Recovery Test Systems and services are actually restored and validated
ACT 03 • VALIDATION PROCESS

Recovery testing follows a controlled process

01
Plan Define the recovery objective
02
Test Execute the recovery procedure
03
Measure Compare results with objectives
04
Improve Correct weaknesses and retest
ACT 04 • VALIDATION CONTROLS

Successful recovery requires measurable results

Data Validation Confirm recovered data is complete and usable.
System Validation Confirm restored systems operate correctly.
Objective Validation Confirm recovery meets required targets.
Failed tests should result in documented corrective actions and another recovery test.
ACT 05 • TESTING RISK

Untested recovery creates uncertainty

Untested Recovery The organization assumes the plan works but does not know whether systems, data, or procedures will perform as expected.
Validated Recovery Recovery procedures are tested, measured, documented, and improved over time.
ACT 06 • PRACTICE

Security+ recovery testing reminders

01 Test recovery procedures regularly
02 Verify recovered systems and data
03 Document failures and corrective actions
04 Retest after recovery procedures change
EXAM MEMORY

Test the plan before you need the plan

Recovery testing validates that procedures, systems, data, and people can actually support recovery during a disruption.

SCENE SUMMARY

What you should remember

Recovery plans should be tested before an actual disaster occurs.
Tabletop exercises, walkthroughs, simulations, and full recovery tests provide different levels of validation.
Recovery results should be measured against defined objectives.
Failed tests should lead to corrective action and additional testing.
FINAL CONCEPT

Recovery is not proven until it is tested

Regular testing and validation turn a recovery plan from a document into a dependable operational capability.
