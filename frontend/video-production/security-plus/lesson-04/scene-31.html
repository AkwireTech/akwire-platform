Data Privacy & Compliance

Privacy and compliance requirements help organizations protect personal information and handle it according to legal, regulatory, and organizational requirements.

ACT 01 • PRIVACY

Personal information requires responsible handling

Privacy focuses on how personal information is collected, used, stored, shared, and ultimately disposed of.

ACT 02 • PRIVACY PRINCIPLES Privacy begins with responsible data practices
Data Minimization Collect only the information that is necessary for a legitimate purpose.
Purpose Limitation Use information for defined and appropriate purposes.
Consent Obtain permission when required before collecting or using personal information.
Transparency Clearly explain how personal information is collected and used.
ACT 03 • PRIVACY LIFECYCLE Privacy controls should follow the information lifecycle
Collect Gather only information that is necessary.
Use Process information for an approved purpose.
Share Share information only when authorized.
Dispose Remove information when retention is no longer required.
ACT 04 • COMPLIANCE Compliance connects security practices to requirements
POLICY Define Requirements
CONTROL Apply Protection
AUDIT Verify Compliance
Compliance requirements vary by organization, industry, location, and the type of information being processed.
ACT 05 • PRIVACY RISK Poor privacy practices can increase exposure
POOR PRACTICES Unnecessary Exposure Excessive collection, unauthorized sharing, and weak retention practices can increase privacy risk.
RESPONSIBLE PRACTICES Controlled Exposure Defined purposes, limited collection, appropriate access, and secure disposal reduce unnecessary exposure.
ACT 06 • PRACTICAL APPLICATION Key privacy and compliance practices
Minimize Collection Avoid collecting information that is not needed.
Protect Access Restrict personal information to authorized users.
Follow Retention Keep information only as long as required.
Verify Compliance Review controls and document compliance activities.
SECURITY+ EXAM MEMORY

Privacy asks how personal information is handled.

When a question involves personal information, think about collection, purpose, consent, access, sharing, retention, disposal, and applicable requirements.

ACT 08 • SCENE SUMMARY Privacy and compliance work together
Collect only necessary information.
Use information for defined purposes.
Restrict access to authorized users.
Verify that required controls are followed.
FINAL CONCEPT

Privacy is about responsible use of information.

Security protects information from threats, while privacy focuses on how personal information is collected, used, shared, retained, and disposed of.
