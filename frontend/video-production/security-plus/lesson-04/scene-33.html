Business Continuity & Disaster Recovery

ACT 01 • CONTINUITY & RECOVERY

Organizations need a plan for unexpected disruptions

Business continuity keeps critical operations running during a disruption, while disaster recovery focuses on restoring systems, data, and services after an incident.

ACT 02 • KEY CONCEPTS

Continuity planning protects critical operations

Business Continuity Keep essential business functions operating
Disaster Recovery Restore systems and services after disruption
Critical Functions Identify what must continue first
Recovery Strategy Define how operations will be restored
ACT 03 • RECOVERY PROCESS

Recovery follows a planned sequence

01
Assess Determine the impact
02
Prioritize Restore critical services first
03
Restore Recover systems and data
04
Validate Confirm secure operations
ACT 04 • RECOVERY PLANNING

Recovery objectives guide the response

RTO Recovery Time Objective defines how quickly a service should be restored.
RPO Recovery Point Objective defines how much data loss is acceptable.
Backups Backups provide recoverable copies of important information.
Recovery plans should be tested regularly so teams know whether the plan actually works.
ACT 05 • PLANNING IMPACT

Preparation affects how quickly operations recover

Unprepared Organization Teams may not know what to restore, who is responsible, or how much disruption can be tolerated.
Prepared Organization Recovery priorities, responsibilities, backups, and recovery objectives are already defined.
ACT 06 • PRACTICE

Security+ continuity reminders

01 Identify critical business functions
02 Define RTO and RPO requirements
03 Maintain and test backups
04 Test and update recovery plans
EXAM MEMORY

RTO = Time • RPO = Data

RTO focuses on how quickly systems should be restored. RPO focuses on how much data loss is acceptable.

SCENE SUMMARY

What you should remember

Business continuity keeps critical operations running during disruption.
Disaster recovery restores systems, services, and data.
RTO defines the required recovery time.
RPO defines the acceptable amount of data loss.
FINAL CONCEPT

Recovery starts before the disaster happens

Effective continuity and recovery planning prepares organizations to protect critical operations, restore services, and reduce the impact of unexpected events.
