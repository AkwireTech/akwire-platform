Data Classification

Data classification identifies the sensitivity and value of information so organizations can apply the appropriate security controls and access requirements.

LESSON 04 • DATA CLASSIFICATION

Not all data requires the same level of protection

Classification helps organizations determine how sensitive information is and which security controls should protect it.

CLASSIFICATION LEVELS Assign protection according to sensitivity
Public Information approved for general access with limited security impact.
Internal Information intended for authorized members of the organization.
Confidential Sensitive information requiring controlled access and protection.
Restricted Highly sensitive information requiring strict authorization and protection.
CLASSIFICATION PROCESS Determine how information should be protected
Identify Determine what information exists.
Classify Assign an appropriate sensitivity level.
Protect Apply controls based on classification.
Review Reassess classification when conditions change.

Sensitive information should receive controls appropriate to its classification, regulatory requirements, and business risk.
CLASSIFICATION RISK Incorrect classification can create security gaps
UNDER-PROTECTION Under-Classification Sensitive information may receive weaker controls than its risk requires.
VS
OVER-RESTRICTION Over-Classification Excessive restrictions can make legitimate access and business operations unnecessarily difficult.
SECURITY+ PRACTICE Four questions for data classification scenarios
What data? Identify the information and its sensitivity.
Who owns it? Determine who is responsible for classification.
Who can access it? Apply access controls based on sensitivity.
Is it still correct? Review classification as business needs change.
EXAM MEMORY

Identify → Classify → Protect → Review

For Security+ questions involving sensitive information, identify the data, determine its classification, apply the appropriate controls, and verify that the classification remains accurate.

LESSON 04 • SCENE 27 SUMMARY Classification connects data sensitivity to protection
Classify information according to its sensitivity and business value.
Apply access and security controls appropriate to the classification.
Under-classification can expose sensitive information.
Review classifications when data, risk, or business requirements change.
