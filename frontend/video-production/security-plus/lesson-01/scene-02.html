Threat, Vulnerability, Risk & Exploit

Learn how security professionals distinguish threats, vulnerabilities, exploits, and risk when evaluating the security of an environment.

Security+ • Fundamental Security Concepts
SECURITY FUNDAMENTALS

Understand the Difference

Security professionals must distinguish between something that could cause harm, a weakness that can be exploited, the method used to exploit that weakness, and the potential impact to the organization.

Threat A potential cause of harm.
Risk Potential for loss or harm.
CONCEPT 01

Threat

Threat Actor

An individual or group that can intentionally or unintentionally cause harm.

EXAMPLES Attacker • Insider • Malware
Threat Event

An occurrence that could negatively affect an information system or organization.

EXAMPLES Data breach • Service disruption
Attack Vector

The path or method used to gain access to a target or deliver an attack.

EXAMPLES Phishing • Compromised service
CONCEPT 02

Vulnerability

A vulnerability is a weakness in a system, application, process, configuration, or human practice that could be exploited and lead to a security impact.

Examples include unpatched software, weak passwords, misconfigurations, and excessive permissions.
CONCEPT 03

Exploit

An exploit is a technique, code, or mechanism that takes advantage of a vulnerability to produce an unintended result or gain unauthorized access.

Think of the vulnerability as the weakness and the exploit as the method used to take advantage of it.
CONCEPT 04

Risk

Likelihood

How likely is it that the threat will exploit the vulnerability?

Impact

What damage or loss could occur if the event happens?

Risk Decision

Security teams use risk information to prioritize appropriate controls.

MEMORY MODEL

How the Concepts Connect

A threat can target a vulnerability. An exploit can take advantage of that vulnerability. If the event creates potential harm to the organization, it becomes a security risk that should be evaluated and managed.

Threat → Vulnerability → Exploit → Potential Impact → Risk
MEMORY CHECK

Remember the Difference

VULNERABILITY Weakness
EXPLOIT Method of exploitation

THREAT Potential source of harm

KEY TAKEAWAY A threat represents a potential source of harm. A vulnerability is a weakness that could be exploited. An exploit is the method used to take advantage of that weakness. Risk represents the potential for loss or harm and helps security professionals prioritize protective measures.
