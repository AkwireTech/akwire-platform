Ethernet Connectors & T568A/T568B Wiring

Identifying 8P8C Ethernet connectors, understanding pin assignments, and recognizing straight-through and crossover wiring.

ETHERNET CONNECTOR

8P8C Modular Connector

Copper Ethernet commonly uses an eight-position, eight-contact modular connector.

8 Positions
8 Contacts
COMMON TERMINOLOGY

Commonly Called RJ45

TECHNICAL FORM 8P8C Eight positions, eight contacts
COMMON NETWORKING TERM RJ45 Widely used name for Ethernet modular plugs

In networking discussions and exam questions, RJ45 is commonly used to describe the modular connector used with twisted-pair Ethernet.

TIA/EIA WIRING STANDARDS

T568A and T568B

STANDARD T568A Green pair begins on pins 1 and 2
OR
STANDARD T568B Orange pair begins on pins 1 and 2

Both standards are valid. The difference is the placement of the orange and green pairs.

KEY DIFFERENCE

Orange and Green Swap Positions

T568B

The blue and brown pairs remain in the same positions. The orange and green pairs change.

Remember the Pattern

SAME Straight-through
A ↔ B Crossover
CONNECTOR 8P8C / RJ45
STANDARDS T568A & T568B

KEY IDEA T568A and T568B define the conductor order inside an Ethernet connector. Use the same standard on both ends for straight-through wiring and A on one end with B on the other for crossover wiring.
