WIRELESS NETWORKING

Communication Without Cables

Wireless networks transmit data using radio frequency signals instead of physical Ethernet cables, allowing devices to communicate while remaining mobile.

Mobility Connect while moving
Flexible Deployment Fewer physical cables

WIRELESS BANDS

2.4 GHz, 5 GHz and 6 GHz

2.4 GHz

Longer range, more interference, fewer non-overlapping channels.

5 GHz

Higher throughput, shorter range, more available channels.

6 GHz

Used by Wi-Fi 6E, providing additional spectrum and reduced congestion.

WIRELESS IDENTIFIERS

SSID, BSSID and ESS

SSID

The Service Set Identifier is the wireless network name users see when selecting a Wi-Fi network.

BSSID

The unique MAC address assigned to a specific wireless access point.

ESS

Multiple access points sharing one SSID create an Extended Service Set.

WIRELESS MODES

Infrastructure vs Ad Hoc

Infrastructure Mode

Wireless devices communicate through an access point.

Ad Hoc Mode

Devices communicate directly with each other without an access point.

PERFORMANCE FACTORS

What Affects Wi-Fi Speed?

Distance Signal strength decreases as devices move farther from the access point.
Interference Other wireless devices and household electronics may reduce performance.
Obstacles Walls, floors and other objects weaken radio signals.
Frequency Band Choosing the correct wireless band affects speed and coverage.
