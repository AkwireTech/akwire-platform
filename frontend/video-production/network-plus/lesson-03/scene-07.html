NETWORK DESIGN

Organizing Modern Networks

A network topology describes how devices connect together, while network types classify networks based on size, purpose, and geographic scope.

Better Design Choose the right layout.
Scalability Support future growth.
TOPOLOGIES

Star vs Bus

Star

Every device connects to a central switch, making troubleshooting simple and reliable.

Bus

All devices share one communication cable. Rarely used today.

TOPOLOGIES

Ring vs Mesh

Ring

Devices form a continuous loop where traffic travels around the ring.

Mesh

Multiple redundant paths provide excellent fault tolerance and availability.

ENTERPRISE DESIGN

Hybrid Topology

Hybrid networks combine multiple topologies, such as star and mesh, to balance performance, scalability, and redundancy.

Most enterprise networks are hybrid.
CONNECTION MODELS

Point-to-Point vs Point-to-Multipoint

Point-to-Point

One dedicated connection between exactly two network devices.

Point-to-Multipoint

One central device communicates with multiple endpoints, common in wireless networks.

NETWORK TYPES

LAN • WAN • MAN • PAN • SAN • VLAN

LAN

Local Area Network serving a home, office, or campus.

WAN

Wide Area Network connecting locations over large geographic distances.

MAN

Metropolitan Area Network spanning a city or region.

PAN

Personal Area Network connecting nearby personal devices.

SAN

Storage Area Network providing high-speed storage connectivity.

VLAN

Virtual LAN logically separates devices without changing physical cabling.
