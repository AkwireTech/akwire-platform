Twisted-Pair Ethernet

Ethernet twisted-pair cable contains copper conductors arranged into pairs that are twisted around each other.

4 twisted pairs
8 individual conductors
WHY TWIST THE WIRES?

Reduce Interference and Crosstalk

Interference
Twisted Pair
Cleaner Signal

Twisting helps cancel electromagnetic interference and reduces crosstalk between neighboring wire pairs.

COPPER CABLE TYPES

UTP vs. STP

UTP

Unshielded Twisted Pair

  • No additional metallic shielding
  • Common in office Ethernet networks
  • Flexible and inexpensive
STP

Shielded Twisted Pair

  • Includes additional shielding
  • Better resistance to EMI
  • Useful in electrically noisy areas
Industrial Equipment
STP
EMI — ELECTROMAGNETIC INTERFERENCE

Environment Matters

Motors, electrical equipment, fluorescent lighting, and power cabling can introduce electromagnetic interference. Shielded cabling can help protect network signals.

CABLE CATEGORIES

Cat 5e, Cat 6, Cat 6a, and Cat 8

Category ratings define performance characteristics for twisted-pair cabling. Higher categories support higher frequencies and newer Ethernet speeds.

CAT 5e 1 Gbps
CAT 6 1 / 10 Gbps
CAT 6a 10 Gbps
CAT 8 25 / 40 Gbps

CABLE JACKET RATINGS

PVC General-purpose cable jacket Used where building codes permit.
PLENUM Low-smoke, fire-resistant jacket Designed for environmental air spaces when required by code.
