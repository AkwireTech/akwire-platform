Wireless Network Devices

Learn the purpose of wireless access points, wireless LAN controllers, wireless routers, repeaters, mesh systems, antennas, Power over Ethernet, and enterprise wireless deployments.

WIRELESS DEVICES

Hardware That Powers Wi-Fi

Wireless networking depends on specialized devices that transmit radio signals, authenticate users, extend coverage, and manage enterprise wireless environments.

Coverage Expands wireless access
Mobility Keeps users connected
DEVICE

Wireless Access Point (WAP)

A Wireless Access Point connects wireless clients to a wired network and provides Wi-Fi coverage.

Central device in most enterprise wireless networks.
ENTERPRISE

Wireless LAN Controller

A Wireless LAN Controller centrally manages multiple access points from a single management interface.

Simplifies enterprise wireless administration.
HOME & SMALL OFFICE

Wireless Router

A wireless router combines routing, switching, NAT, DHCP, firewall services, and an integrated wireless access point.

Commonly used in homes and small businesses.
COVERAGE

Repeaters & Range Extenders

Repeaters receive an existing wireless signal and retransmit it to increase network coverage.

Larger coverage, but may reduce available throughput.
MODERN DEPLOYMENT

Mesh Wi-Fi System

Multiple mesh nodes work together to provide seamless wireless coverage throughout a building.

Ideal for eliminating wireless dead zones.
ANTENNAS

Omnidirectional vs Directional

Omnidirectional

Broadcasts wireless signals in all directions.

Directional

Focuses the wireless signal toward a specific direction.

POWER

Power over Ethernet (PoE)

PoE delivers electrical power and network connectivity through a single Ethernet cable.

Simplifies installation of ceiling-mounted access points.
DEPLOYMENT

Standalone vs Controller-Based

Standalone AP

Each access point is configured individually.

Controller-Based

One Wireless LAN Controller manages many access points.
