LESSON REVIEW

Key Topics Covered

Documentation

Network diagrams, inventories, topology maps, and operational documentation.

Monitoring

Baselines, SNMP, Syslog, NetFlow, performance analysis, and monitoring tools.

CONFIGURATION

Configuration Management

Remember the differences between running and startup configurations, configuration backups, version control, maintenance windows, and rollback plans.

Always maintain recoverable configurations.
RESILIENCE

Disaster Recovery & High Availability

Understand Disaster Recovery, Business Continuity, RTO, RPO, redundancy, clustering, load balancing, RAID, and fault tolerance.

Remove single points of failure whenever possible.
OPERATIONS

Policies & Operational Procedures

Review Acceptable Use Policies, password policies, BYOD, remote access, SOPs, documentation, and incident response. These administrative controls support secure, reliable, and repeatable network operations.

Good documentation and standardized procedures reduce operational risk.
NETWORK+ EXAM

Exam Preparation Tips

Focus on understanding operational concepts instead of memorizing isolated definitions. Expect scenario-based questions that require selecting the best operational practice for maintaining secure and reliable networks.

Read every exam question carefully before selecting an answer.
